Cheat sheet

Required. Runs once per bar, oldest first.
def setup(acct): / def on_finish(acct):
Optional. Run before the first bar and after the last.
bar.date bar.price bar.open bar.high bar.low bar.index
Today's bar. bar.date is a Python date; bar.index counts from 0 at your start date.
acct.cash acct.ounces acct.value acct.price
What you have right now, at today's price.
acct.avg_cost acct.gold_share acct.profit
Average price paid per ounce held, the fraction of your money in gold, and profit so far.
acct.history(n) · acct.history(n, "high")
The last n closing prices (or "open", "high", "low"), oldest first, today last. It reaches back before your start date, so indicators are ready on day one.
acct.buy(dollars=1000) · acct.buy(ounces=2) · acct.buy_all()
Buy gold. Capped at the cash you have.
acct.sell(ounces=1) · acct.sell(dollars=500) · acct.sell_all()
Sell gold. Capped at what you hold. No shorting or borrowing.
acct.target(0.6)
Buy or sell so 60% of the account is in gold.
note="why"
Any order can carry a note. It shows in the trades list and on the chart.
acct.trades · acct.last_trade · acct.pending
Your fills so far (each has .date .side .ounces .price .pnl) and orders waiting for the next bar.

Indicator helpers, ready to use. They return None until there are enough values.
