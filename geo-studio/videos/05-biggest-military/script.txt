Who has the biggest military on Earth? Let's start with the smallest.
Costa Rica: zero. It abolished its army in 1948.
Vatican City: 135 Swiss Guards. The smallest army in the world.
Luxembourg: about 1,000 soldiers.
Ireland: 7,500.
The United Kingdom: around 140,000.
Turkey: 355,000. The second biggest in NATO.
Pakistan: 660,000.
Russia: about 1.1 million.
North Korea: 1.28 million, out of just 26 million people.
The United States: 1.3 million.
India: almost 1.5 million.
And number one: China, with over 2 million active soldiers.
But size isn't everything. America spends almost a trillion dollars a year on its military. More than the next nine countries combined.
