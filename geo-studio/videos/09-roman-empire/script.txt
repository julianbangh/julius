What if the Roman Empire came back today?
At its peak in 117 AD, Rome ruled 5 million square kilometers, from Britain to Egypt.
Bring those borders back, and Rome swallows more than 40 modern countries.
London, Paris, Madrid, Istanbul, Cairo and Jerusalem are all Roman again.
That's over half a billion people. More than the entire European Union.
Its economy: about 16 trillion dollars. Third biggest on Earth, behind only the US and China.
And it's a nuclear power, with France and Britain's warheads.
But there's a problem. Who's in charge? Rome, or the old eastern capital, Istanbul? Paris wants a say. So does London.
Within a year, the empire splits in two... exactly like last time.
